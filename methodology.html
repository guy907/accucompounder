Methodology

1. Universe

ETFs: only European accumulating UCITS. Stocks: only accumulating US + EU growers (no dividend-chasers). Long-only, no leverage.

2. Consolidated Advisors

Stock Advisor (11 tickers tracked, e.g. WAT / HALO / ARW top conviction) + ETF Advisor (73 tickers, 5 BUY / 22 ACCUMULATE). Dozens of signals → one weighted score → HOLD/BUY/TRIM/SELL + human check.

3. Honest reporting

Weights in %, losers visible (e.g. XCHA.DE -3.34%, ASWC.DE -5.29%, DKNG -12.37%), winners visible (USPY.DE +54.33%, HALO +52.56%). Benchmark MSCI World / S&P 500 always alongside.

4. Risk & disclaimer

Educational showcase only. Not financial advice. Position caps, sector-concentration guard (e.g. Technology 45% flagged). Regime audit log-only, never a trade trigger.
